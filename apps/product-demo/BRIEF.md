## Story

Follow one synthetic fibre visit: Sales routes a client to a squad; a lead assigns a job; the engineer accepts, checks in, scans the cable label and captures answers by voice; a confirmed report queues offline, syncs when reachable, and receives human review. Show organisation discovery/messaging, scoped roles, time tracking, reusable forms and dashboard AI with source links.

## Truthfulness

Use actual existing screenshots for product visuals, clearly synthetic sample conversations and illustrative workflow overlays. Do not invent live integrations, SLA improvements or closed-app push. Initial provisioning needs connectivity; local voice/OCR works after provisioning; queued delivery resumes with app open/unlocked and reachable server. Receipt is delivery, not approval. Dashboard AI is online and advisory. Phone build 2014 has a reported recognition regression; source accuracy repair awaits a requested APK. This film explains capabilities and is not new physical acceptance evidence.

## Creative decisions

No third-party service sign-in, publishing, live microphone or APK generation required. Infer pacing and scene details from the user's end-to-end presentation goal. Keep source and asset provenance with the project.
